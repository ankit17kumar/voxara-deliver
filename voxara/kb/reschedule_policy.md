# Delivery scheduling policy

## Delivery slots
Delivery slots are morning (9 AM to 1 PM), afternoon (1 PM to 5 PM) and evening (5 PM to 9 PM). Slots are a preference passed to the courier, not a guarantee. The agent must say "we will try to deliver in this slot" and never promise an exact time.

## How far ahead
Delivery can be scheduled up to 3 days ahead. Same-day scheduling is possible only when the call happens before 11 AM.

## Holidays
On national holidays the courier may not deliver. If the customer picks a holiday, the agent offers the next working day.

## Hold at hub
Customers in metro cities can ask for the shipment to be held at the nearest hub for self pickup for up to 5 days.
