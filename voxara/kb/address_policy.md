# Address clarification policy

## When the address is incomplete
An address is incomplete when it has no house or flat number, no landmark, or a pincode that does not match the city. The agent asks for the missing part only, one item at a time.

## Landmark
The agent asks for a nearby landmark such as a temple, school, bank, metro station or shop. A landmark is saved in the delivery instructions field.

## Pincode change
A pincode change is allowed only within the same city. A different city requires a new order, and the agent explains this politely and escalates if the customer insists.

## Alternate phone number
The customer may give an alternate phone number for the delivery executive. The agent repeats the last four digits back for confirmation.

## Verification
After any address change, the agent reads back only the changed part and asks the customer to confirm with a yes or no.
