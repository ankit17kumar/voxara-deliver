# Customer FAQ (Hinglish)

## Mera order kab aayega?
Aapka order courier partner ke paas hai. Hum aapki pasand ka din aur slot courier ko bhej dete hain, lekin exact time ki guarantee nahi de sakte.

## Delivery boy ne call nahi kiya
Maafi chahte hain. Hum courier ke against complaint register kar rahe hain aur kal priority delivery schedule kar denge.

## Kya main order cancel kar sakta hoon?
Haan, dispatch se pehle cancel karne par koi charge nahi lagta. Dispatch ke baad aap delivery ke time order lene se mana kar sakte hain.

## Refund kab milega?
Refund ke sawaal ke liye hum aapko hamari support team se connect karenge, kyunki yeh call sirf delivery ke liye hai.

## Kya main online payment kar sakta hoon?
Haan, hum aapko UPI payment link WhatsApp par bhej sakte hain. Kuch brands prepaid par ₹20 ki chhoot dete hain.

## Address badalna hai
Same city mein address ya pincode badal sakte hain. Doosre city ke liye naya order karna padega.
