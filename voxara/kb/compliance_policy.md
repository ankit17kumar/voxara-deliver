# Calling compliance policy (India)

## Calling hours
Calls are placed only between 9 AM and 9 PM IST. Calls outside this window are queued for the next morning.

## Identity and disclosure
Every call starts by naming the brand and saying that this is an automated assistant calling about the customer's order. The call is transactional and must not include any promotional offer.

## Recording and consent
The agent says the call may be recorded for quality. If the customer objects, recording stops and the call continues or is handed to a human.

## Data protection
The agent never reads out the full address, the full phone number or payment details. Transcripts are stored with phone numbers masked. Customer data is deleted per the brand's retention setting, 90 days by default, in line with the DPDP Act.

## Attempts
A customer is called at most twice per NDR event, at least 3 hours apart. If the customer says "do not call", the number is suppressed for that order.
