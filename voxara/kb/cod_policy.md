# COD (Cash on Delivery) confirmation policy

## Purpose
COD orders are confirmed by a call before dispatch to reduce RTO. An order that is not confirmed within 24 hours of placement is held, not cancelled, and a WhatsApp reminder is sent.

## What the agent confirms
The agent confirms the product name, the order amount and the delivery address pincode. The agent never reads out the full address or the full phone number on the call; it reads only the pincode and the landmark.

## Switching to prepaid
The agent may offer a prepaid UPI link once. Prepaid orders get a ₹20 discount when the brand has enabled it. If the customer declines, the agent does not repeat the offer.

## Cancellation
If the customer says they did not place the order or no longer want it, the agent cancels the order and confirms the cancellation. No cancellation fee applies before dispatch.

## Amount questions
If the customer disputes the amount, the agent explains that the amount includes product price, taxes and any COD fee shown at checkout. If the customer still disagrees, the agent escalates to a human.
