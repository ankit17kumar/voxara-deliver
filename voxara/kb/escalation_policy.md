# Escalation and human handoff policy

## When the agent hands off to a human
The agent transfers the call, or creates a callback task, when: the customer asks for a human; the customer is angry for two turns in a row; the customer is very angry and the agent cannot act on what they asked; the customer mentions a legal threat, fraud, a refund dispute or a damaged product; the agent's confidence stays low for two turns; or the request is outside the delivery workflow.

## What the handoff carries
Every handoff carries the order ID, the NDR reason, the transcript so far, the detected emotion trend and the customer's last request, so the human never asks the customer to repeat themselves.

## Callback
If no human is free, the agent promises a callback within 2 working hours and creates a task. The agent never promises a time it cannot guarantee.

## Tone during handoff
The agent acknowledges the customer's feeling first, apologises for the trouble, and says clearly what will happen next.
